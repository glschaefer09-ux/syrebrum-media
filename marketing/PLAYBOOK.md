# Syrebrum Social Playbook (v1, 2026-10-29)

For the posting agent and its reviewers. Sources: [S#] at the end.

## 0. Lessons from the Series B brands

- **Maven AGI:** "How [customer] did X" case studies with timed results ("25% more solves/hour in week one"); "up to" hedging; awards; "[Vendor] pricing" posts; one CTA, "Book a demo". [S1–S3]
- **Decagon:** Separate tone and cadence for LinkedIn, X and Instagram; ghostwritten executive posts; launch videos with three bullets, one link and weekly follow-ups; case studies led by two big numbers and a named operator's quote. [S4–S7]
- **SentinelOne:** One category idea (AI makes the call, not a spec-sheet feature); outside proof (MITRE 100% detection); employees reshare news; a creator makes platform-native shorts (talking heads, skits, trends) while YouTube holds full demos. [S8–S12]
- **Clay:** Bold claim → what we did instead → workflow → number → invite; "How Clay uses Clay"; no stock images. [S13–S14]

**We copy:** sourced price math, one CTA, per-channel copy, launches as series, one repeated promise, shorts plus YouTube demos, "How CCD runs on Syrebrum".

(Their LinkedIn/X feeds block crawlers; evidence is their sites, YouTube, job posts and analyses.)

## 1. Positioning

**Core line:** Your back office, run by the AI you already pay for. Nothing goes out without your OK.

**Real features:**
- **Job board:** request to paid; confirmations wait for your OK; service plans.
- **Quotes and payments:** quote to invoice in one tap; overdue reminders drafted; cash or check recorded on the phone.
- **Inventory:** stock, markup, margin, low-stock flags, warranty dates.
- **Phone app:** schedule, approvals, money, AI chat.
- **Bring your own AI:** works with Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini.
- **Packs:** Roofing and HVAC in Pro and Premier; Small Business in every plan.
- **IT checkup:** read-only, health graphs, alerts, ALL STOP; "a checkup, not a guarantee, not antivirus".
- **Pricing:** Basic $29.99, Pro $49.99, Premier $99.99, with a 14-day free trial.

**Holds:**
- Leads & follow-ups: "coming in the next update" until a reviewer confirms release.
- Syrebrum Ops: "preview", never "included".
- No per-plan PC counts; no Mesh, IT Department Pack, iOS or Tap to Pay.
- Not "replaces Notion/Slack"; say "one flat price instead of per-seat tools".

## 2. Pillars and weekly mix (7 posts)

Templates: **Value prop ×3**: hook (≤8 words), one feature, "you OK it", link. **Pain ×1**: "If you [pain], [cost]", fix, link. **Clip ×1**: "Watch: [task] in [N]s", fictional-data note, link. **Founder ×1**: bold claim, why, question (Graham's LinkedIn, his approval). **Proof ×1**: verifiable number, source, so-what, link.

**Drafts** (each ends with https://syrebrum.com):

- **Value prop**
  1. Jobs, invoices, stock, approvals: one app, your AI, your OK.
  2. Approved quote → invoice in one tap. Overdue reminders drafted for you.
  3. Already paying for Claude or ChatGPT? Put it to work on your jobs.
- **Customer pain**
  1. Invoicing at 10 p.m.? That's the shift Syrebrum takes.
  2. A job request buried in a text thread is a lost job. One board, one tap.
  3. Disk full, found out too late? A weekly checkup tells you first.
- **Product clip** (each one says "Demo data is fictional.")
  1. Watch: request to paid in 27 seconds.
  2. Watch: a cash payment recorded on site in two taps.
  3. Watch: a roofer's storm lead becomes an estimate.
- **Founder**
  1. I won't let an AI text my customers without me, so every message waits for your OK. Would you trust an AI with yours?
  2. Small shops shouldn't pay twice for AI. Syrebrum uses the subscription you have. What do you pay today?
  3. We run our own company on Syrebrum. Here's this week's board.
- **Proof/stat**
  1. 5 people on Notion, Slack and hosted MCP: $165.25/mo. Syrebrum Pro: $49.99 (list prices, 28 Oct 2026; sources in comments).
  2. Approvals are tied to the exact message. Edit it and it needs a new OK. ALL STOP blocks every send.
  3. About 2,400 automated tests run on the Syrebrum app (counted 28 Oct 2026).

## 4. Rules

1. No invented stats, testimonials, reviews, customer counts or logos. Name a customer only with their written OK and Graham's approval.
2. Demo footage carries "Demo data is fictional."
3. Every post includes https://syrebrum.com. On Instagram and TikTok, write "syrebrum.com (link in bio)".
4. Affiliate/referral links (Metricool, Ramp) carry "Affiliate link: we may earn a commission."
5. Every post goes through `createScheduledPostForReview` and is reviewed before it goes out.
6. Fresh copy per channel; never identical text on two.
7. Each clip's .md "What's real" notes cap claims.
